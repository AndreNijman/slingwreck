## One case the original spec did not cover

Three stars was defined as "better than the bot's best". On a single-solution level, best
and median coincide and that threshold becomes unreachable — an unwinnable third star is a
worse outcome than a soft one.

So for levels where the bot's best and median are within a few percent, set three stars at
the bot's best plus a **small** margin derived from what is still destructible in the level
— something a human can reach by finishing off structure the bot left standing. If even
that is not reachable, set it to the bot's best and say which levels those are.

Whatever you choose, state the rule in the `levels.js` comment alongside the main formula.
These numbers need a provenance.
