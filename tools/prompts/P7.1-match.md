## P7.1 — Siege scoring

`SCORE.siege` has been stubbed in `sim.js` since P2.1 with a comment pointing here.
Implement it exactly as `docs/DESIGN.md` §6.3 specifies: block value destroyed, the extra
bonus for a block knocked entirely off the plot, per-pig values, unused critters, and the
**breach bonus** when the King has clear line of sight from the slingshot arc at round end.

The breach test is a raycast, the same shape as the burial-depth check in `build.js`. Reuse
that logic rather than writing a second cone-of-sight test.

## P7.2 — Match structure

Best of five, first to three wins. Per §6.3, a round ends when a King pops (that player
wins the round immediately), when both bags are spent and both worlds have settled, or when
the 3-minute clock expires. If no King popped, the higher siege score takes the round.

Sudden death on an exact tie: one Lob each at the same fortresses, higher damage wins.
Still tied, the player whose fortress cost less scrap wins — which terminates and rewards
efficiency.

## P7.3 — Scrap budgets

`budget = BUDGET.base + BUDGET.perRound * (round - 1) + BUDGET.perDeficit * roundsBehind`,
plus banked early-lock scrap at `BUDGET.earlyLockPer10s` per remaining 10 seconds, plus
`BUDGET.winnerBonus` to the round winner.

## P7.4 — The draft

The **loser** of a round picks one of three cards; the winner gets scrap and nothing else.
Tier comes from `CARD_TIER_RULES` in `data.js`, which is keyed on deficit — and note the
comment there recording that the design's separate "down by 2 at match point" case was
unreachable in a first-to-three match and was removed.

The relay rolls the three candidates from the **match seed** so the draft is reproducible
and auditable. No duplicate cards for a player. 25-second timer, with a default pick if it
expires — never leave a match stalled on an away player.

## P7.5 — Card effects

All 25, applied from their **declarative `effect` data**. No switch statement on card id.
The effects were authored as data in P3.2 specifically so this stays table-driven, and every
one has its numbers recorded with reasoning beside it.

Categories to handle: `unlock`, `materialCost`, `budget`, `bagSize`, `ammoPool`, `pigHp`,
`plotRow`, `previewRate`, `decoyKing`, `pigAbility`, `headwind`, `bonusShot`,
`restoreBlock`, `materialAbility`, `slingPull`, `kingBalloon`, `remoteTnt`, `slingshots`,
`ammoHoming`, `plotTilt`, `autoPig`.

Two that need care because they touch the wire:

- `decoyKing` and `flak-hog` mark a **specific placed pig** via the pig-tuple flags added
  in P4.1b. That path exists; use it.
- `previewRate` changes the opponent's view of your fortress, so it is enforced by the
  **relay**, not by the client that benefits from it.

Anything a card grants must be validated server-side. A client claiming iron it never
drafted is the obvious attack.

## Tests — `tools/siege-test.mjs`

Headless, measured, PASS/FAIL with numbers, non-zero exit:

1. Scoring: each component in isolation, and the breach bonus firing only when the King is
   actually exposed.
2. Round resolution: King pop beats a higher score; higher score wins when no King pops;
   sudden death terminates; the cheaper-fortress tie-break resolves.
3. Budgets: the formula at rounds 1 to 5 at each deficit, and early-lock banking.
4. Draft: tier selection at every deficit, no duplicates, reproducible from a seed, and the
   default pick on timeout.
5. **All 25 cards**: one assertion each that the card measurably changes what it claims to.
   A card that does nothing is a bug and this is the only thing that will catch it — the
   same standard the abilities were held to in P3.3.
6. A full best-of-five played headlessly between two bots, asserting the match terminates
   with a winner and that the draft only ever went to the round loser.
