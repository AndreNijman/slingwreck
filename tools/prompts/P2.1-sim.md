## Scope

`sim.js` is the game on top of the solver. This task covers everything **except the
critter abilities**, which arrive in P3 — for now only `nib` (no ability) needs to
actually fly. Structure the ability dispatch so P3 fills in a table, and leave the
eight unimplemented abilities as clearly-marked entries that throw if invoked, rather
than silently doing nothing. A silent no-op ability is indistinguishable from a bug.

**Do not write any rendering, DOM, network or audio code.** `sim.js` must stay
importable by Node and by a Cloudflare Worker. `tools/check.mjs` fails the build if it
references a browser global, `Math.random`, `Date`, or any banned `Math.*`.

## The damage hook

`physics.js` has a `damage()` slot in its step pipeline, currently a no-op, and it must
stay ignorant of pigs and scores. Add **one** seam to `physics.js`: `makeWorld` accepts
an optional `onDamage` callback, and the `damage` pipeline slot calls
`world.onDamage(world)` when one is set. `sim.js` supplies it. That is the only change
you may make to `physics.js`.

Contacts carry their accumulated `pn` and `pt` after the velocity solve, which is
exactly what the impulse damage model in DESIGN.md §2.4 needs.

### Blueprint format — this is wire format, design it to be small

```js
{ v: 1,
  blocks: [ [shapeId, matId, x, y, rot], ... ],   // rot in 15-degree steps, integer
  pigs:   [ [pigId, x, y], ... ] }
```

Arrays rather than objects because a 120-block blueprint crosses the wire every round
and object keys would triple it. Provide named accessor constants for the tuple
indices so the rest of the file never indexes with a bare number.

### Round state

A flat object holding at least: `world`, `blueprint`, `seed`, `rng`, `bag` (array of
ammo ids), `shotIndex`, `shots` (the log the relay replays: `{ step, ammo, dx, dy, tapStep }`),
`phase`, `time`, `stepCount`, `events`, `score`, `pigs`, `blocks`, `flying`,
`settleTimer`.

`phase` is one of `'aiming'`, `'flying'`, `'settling'`, `'won'`, `'lost'`.

### The slingshot

`launch(round, dx, dy)` takes the draw vector from the anchor at
`(TUNE.slingX, TUNE.slingY)`, clamps its length to `TUNE.slingRadius`, and launches the
next critter in the bag **opposite** the draw direction at a speed proportional to draw
fraction, up to `TUNE.launchSpeedMax`. Add `launchSpeedMax: 30` to `TUNE` in `data.js` —
that is the only change you may make to `data.js`. It is derived, not guessed: reaching
the far edge of the plot from the sling is about 40 world units, and `sqrt(40 * 22)` is
29.7, so 30 covers the plot with a little margin. Say so in a comment.

Reject a launch when the phase is not `'aiming'` or the bag is spent.

### Damage

Exactly DESIGN.md §2.4:

```
dmg = max(0, normalImpulse - mat.thresh) * mat.frailty
```

plus `TUNE.brittleTangentFactor * max(0, tangentImpulse - thresh)` for brittle
materials. Pigs use their own `thresh` and `frailty` and their directional armour
traits (`helm` above, `tusk` from the sling side) reduce incoming damage by
`armourFraction` when the contact normal points the armoured way.

A body at or below zero hp is marked dead and removed **at the end of the step, never
during it** — removing a body while the contact list still references it is how a
solver starts reading freed state. Emit an event for each.

Explosions: radial impulse and direct damage, both falling off linearly to zero at the
radius, with a `raycast` occlusion check per body so a stone wall shields what is
behind it. **Chained TNT must resolve over successive steps via a pending queue, never
by recursion** — a five-crate cluster must not be able to overflow the stack.

### Events

`stepRound` returns an array of plain data events for `render.js` and `audio.js` to
consume: `{ kind: 'shatter', x, y, mat, shape }`, `{ kind: 'pop', pig, x, y }`,
`{ kind: 'boom', x, y, r }`, `{ kind: 'hit', x, y, impulse, mat }`,
`{ kind: 'launch', ammo }`, `{ kind: 'settled' }`, `{ kind: 'won' }`, `{ kind: 'lost' }`.
`sim.js` never calls a renderer; it returns data.

### Ending a round

Won when every pig is dead. Lost when the bag is spent, the world has settled
(`isSettled`, with `TUNE.settleTimeout` as a hard cap), and a pig still lives. After a
shot the phase goes `'flying'` then `'settling'`, and back to `'aiming'` once settled
and the round is not over.

### Scoring

Campaign: `SCORE.campaign` — `10000` per unused critter, `5000` per pig, `100 × cost`
of destroyed blocks. Siege scoring per `SCORE.siege` may be stubbed with a comment
pointing at P7; do not invent the breach test now.

## tools/sim-test.mjs

Headless assertions, PASS/FAIL lines with measured values, non-zero exit on failure:

1. A launch at full draw leaves the sling at `TUNE.launchSpeedMax` within 1%, and a
   half draw at half that.
2. A launch aimed 45 degrees up lands within the plot — proves the tuning actually
   reaches the fortress, which is the single most likely tuning mistake.
3. A glass block dies to an impulse above its threshold and survives one below it.
4. Damage is zero for a resting stack over 300 steps. **A tower must not damage itself
   by standing there**, and this is the assertion that catches a `thresh` that is too low.
5. A five-TNT cluster fully chains without recursion and every crate dies.
6. Explosion damage behind a stone wall is strictly less than the same explosion with
   the wall removed — proves occlusion works.
7. A round with one pig and one critter reaches `'won'`; the same round with the pig
   moved out of reach reaches `'lost'` with the bag spent.
8. Two rounds built from the same `(blueprint, seed, shot log)` produce the same
   `digestRound` after replay. **This is the relay audit model in miniature and it is
   the most important assertion in the file.**
