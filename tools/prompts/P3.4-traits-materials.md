Implement the remaining pig traits and material behaviours in `sim.js`. Read
`ARCHITECTURE.md` §3, `data.js`, `sim.js`, and `docs/DESIGN.md` §2.2 and §4.

Everything here is already authored as data and currently does nothing. This task makes
each one real, and adds a **measured** assertion for each to `tools/sim-test.mjs`
comparing the behaviour present against the behaviour absent, printing both numbers.

## Pig traits

| trait | behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| `helm` armour | already has `armourFrom: 'above'`, `armourFraction: 0.6`. Damage arriving with a contact normal pointing downward onto the pig is reduced by 60%. **Verify it from both sides** — a hit from above must be reduced and a hit from the side must not. Armour tested from one direction is not tested. |
| `tusk` armour | `armourFrom: 'sling'`. Reduced by 60% when the contact normal comes from the slingshot side, full damage from behind or above. Same two-sided verification. |
| `zep` balloon | the pig hangs from a balloon: a separate small body above it, joined so the pig hovers, drifting ±`driftRange` over `driftSeconds`. Pop the balloon and the pig falls, and the fall usually finishes it. |
| `sarge` repair | every `repairEvery` seconds, restore `repairFraction` of one adjacent damaged block's max hp. **Siege only** — inert in campaign mode. Which block must be deterministic: nearest first, ties broken by body id. |

The drift is a position over time and must not use trigonometry. A triangle wave, or a
piecewise polynomial, is fine and is arithmetic; `Math.sin` is banned and
`tools/check.mjs` will fail the build.

## Material behaviours

| material | behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| `spring` | `ammoRestitution: 0.92` against critters, its ordinary `restitution: 0.2` against everything else. |
| `gel` | `absorb: 0.70`. Gel takes its own damage normally, but a body on the far side of a gel block receives only 30% of the impulse-derived damage it otherwise would. This is the anti-Lob block: it protects what is behind it without being tough itself. |
| `sand` | `chunks: 3`. At zero hp it becomes three smaller dynamic bodies that **keep the original total mass**, rather than vanishing. |
| large `stone` | stone pieces of area 2 or more split into two halves along the long axis instead of vanishing, again keeping total mass. |

Sub-body placement must be deterministic: fixed offsets inside the original footprint,
ids from the world counter in a fixed order. No randomness, not even seeded, because
there is no reason for any here.

## One generic addition to physics.js

`spring` needs a restitution that depends on *which pair* is touching, and the solver
currently uses `max(a.rest, b.rest)`. Add a hook in the same shape as the existing
`onDamage`: `makeWorld` accepts an optional `restitutionFor(a, b)`, and the contact
setup calls it when present, falling back to the max rule. `sim.js` supplies the one
that knows about springs and critters.

`physics.js` must not learn what a spring is. It calls an opaque callback.

## Rules

- No `Math.*` beyond the permitted list anywhere in `sim.js`, `physics.js` or `data.js`.
- Anything that spawns bodies takes ids from the world counter so shot logs replay
  identically.
- Emit events for `render.js` and `audio.js`: a balloon popping, a repair, a sandbag
  crumbling, and a spring launch are all things the player must be able to see and hear.
  `render.js` does not have to draw them yet; the events must exist.
- Sarge's repair changes the world on a timer rather than on an impact, so make sure it
  cannot resurrect a block whose space is now occupied, and that it wakes the island.
