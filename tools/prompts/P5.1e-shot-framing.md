Fix the framing in `tools/level-shots.mjs`. The tool works; the images do not let anyone
review a level, which is the only reason it exists.

## The measurement

From `shots/levels/_contact-sheet.png`: each tile is about 316×175 px and frames the
slingshot at x −9 through the far edge of the plot at x 24 — 33 world units — so roughly
**9 px per world unit**. `sty-01` is a four-piece hut occupying maybe 60×30 px of a
316×175 tile. Most of every tile is sky and empty ground.

This is the third time on this project that a camera has been pointed at the whole
playfield instead of at the subject: the aiming camera in P2, the editor in P4, and now
this. The pattern is that framing "everything" reliably means framing mostly nothing.

## The fix

**Frame the structure, not the plot.** Compute the bounding box of the placed pieces after
the settle, add a margin of about 1.5 world units, and fit that. Then:

- Enforce a **minimum framed width** of around 10 world units, so a four-piece tutorial
  level is not absurdly magnified and levels stay roughly comparable to each other at a
  glance. Comparability across tiles is the whole point of a contact sheet.
- Keep the ground line visible and near the bottom of the frame — a structure floating in
  the middle of a tile reads wrongly.
- Keep the slingshot as the compact edge marker with its distance, the same one the editor
  uses. Direction and distance are part of reading a level; a full view of the sling is
  not.

Report the new pixels-per-unit for the tightest and widest level.

## Two smaller things

**`moved` is currently a boolean and it reads `YES` for every level**, including one that
moved 0.00018 units. That is not information. Print the **number**, and only flag it when
it exceeds something worth a human's attention — a tenth of the settle tolerance is a
reasonable line. A column that says YES for everything gets ignored, which defeats having
it.

**Make the contact sheet grid adapt.** Three tiles across a 696 px sheet is fine; 52 will
not be. Choose the column count from the level count so a full campaign is a readable
sheet rather than a 20 000-pixel column, and support `--episode` producing a sheet of just
that episode's 13.
