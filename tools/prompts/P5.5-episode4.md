## What this episode adds

Per `EPISODES` in `data.js`: `hulk` and `zip` join the bag; **Sarge** joins the pigs;
**iron** and **gel** join the materials. Everything from Episodes 1 to 3 carries over.

Note: `EPISODES` mentions "moving platforms". **There are none in the simulation** and you
must not invent them. The episode's identity is iron, gel and the two new critters.

Note: Sarge's repair is **Siege only** — it is inert in campaign mode. So Sarge here is
simply a 5 hp pig, and levels must not depend on repair behaviour.

Teaching order:

| levels | job |
| --- | --- |
| 1–3 | iron. Nearly indestructible, so the lesson is to stop attacking it and go around, under, or drop it |
| 4–6 | `hulk`. Wedge it into a gap and inflate; note it can also be tapped at rest, which is often better |
| 7–9 | gel. It absorbs 70% of what passes through it, so a level should punish attacking a gel-shielded face and reward finding another way |
| 10–12 | `zip` and Sarge. Zip blinks past a barrier; Sarge is a 5 hp wall of pig that needs real force |
| 13 | the campaign finale. Iron core, gel shielding, Sarge, and every critter in the game needed |

## Note added after a failed attempt

A previous attempt on a weaker model produced 13 levels that failed **every** gate:
out-of-bounds, locked materials, pigs dying during the settle, movement of 0.034 to 1.158
against the 0.02 requirement, codec round trips changing bytes, and blocks off the 0.25
grid. It was reverted.

Two lessons from it:

- **Compose from `motifs.js`.** That attempt hand-placed long runs of identical cubes,
  which is how you get 191 pieces that do not settle. The motifs already carry their own
  settle assertions.
- **`locked-material` is a real rule.** A campaign level may only use what its episode has
  unlocked, and Episode 4 unlocks gel via the `gelworks` card — check how Episode 3
  handles `springloaded` in `levels.js` and follow that pattern.

Run `node tools/level-export.mjs --lint` and `node tools/level-audit.mjs` **as you go**,
not once at the end. Both are fast and both will tell you immediately.
