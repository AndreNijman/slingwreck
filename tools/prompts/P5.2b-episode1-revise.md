Revise Episode 1. The escalation you built is real and the per-level ideas are sound —
keep both. But the contact sheet shows two problems that go to the premise of the game,
and they are worth fixing in 13 levels now rather than in 52 later.

## 1. The pigs are not protected, so the structures do not matter

Look at the sheet again. In almost every level the pig sits in the open — underneath a
gantry, or perched on top of a plank — with a clear line from the slingshot. `sty-02` is
the plainest case: three pieces with the Runt sitting on top, hittable directly with the
first `nib`, structure untouched.

If a pig can be hit directly, **the fortress is decoration**. The entire tension of this
genre is that the pig is protected and you have to work out the way in: which support is
load-bearing, which pane is the weak point, what falls onto what. A level where the
correct play is "aim at the pig" has no idea in it, whatever the structure looks like.

Fix:

- Levels 1 and 2 may keep a directly hittable pig. They are teaching aiming and arc, and
  a first success should be simple.
- **From level 3 onward, every pig must require the structure to be affected.** Enclose
  it, roof it, put a pane in front of it, or place it where only a collapse or a ricochet
  reaches it. Verify it rather than assuming: cast a ray from the sling along a plausible
  arc and confirm a block is in the way.
- By levels 10–13, at least one pig per level should need **two events** — a support
  broken *and* something falling — not a single lucky shot.

## 2. Almost every structure is a table

Counting from the sheet: 01, 02, 03, 08, 09, 10, 12 and 13 are all two legs with a
horizontal top. That is one idea repeated eight times, and it makes the episode read as
variations on a trestle.

`motifs.js` has `bunker`, `keep`, `stack` and `scaffold` and the sheet shows almost none
of them. Widen the vocabulary:

- an **enclosed bunker** with the pig genuinely inside, where the way in is a specific wall
- a **tower** the player topples, rather than a top the player drops
- a **stacked wall** that must be breached rather than knocked over
- a pig **behind a glass pane** so the pane is the puzzle
- a structure that is deliberately **unbalanced**, so hitting it high tips it

At most three of the thirteen should be gantries.

## 3. The plot is 24 wide and you are using about 8 of it

Every structure sits centred in roughly the middle third. Nothing uses distance.

Give at least three levels **two targets at meaningfully different ranges**, so the player
has to change trajectory rather than repeat one shot. That is a different skill from
choosing a critter and the episode currently never asks for it.

Also: the tallest level is 9.6 units of an available 16. The last two levels can go to
11–12 and should.

## Keep

- The monotonic height escalation. It works.
- The teaching order — `nib` alone, then `chip` on glass, then `wedge` on wood, then
  combinations.
- The one-sentence idea per level, and keep them as comments.
- `sty-07`, the four-piece precision level. It is the best level in the episode: minimal,
  legible, and the whole thing hinges on one spine.
