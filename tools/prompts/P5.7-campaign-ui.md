## Episode and level select

Reached from the title screen. Two levels of navigation:

- **Episode picker** — the four episodes from `EPISODES`, each showing its name, theme,
  what it introduces, and progress as stars earned out of 39. An episode is locked until
  the previous one has at least 8 of its 13 levels completed, so a stuck player is never
  hard-blocked.
- **Level grid** — the 13 levels of an episode, each tile showing its number, name, and
  stars earned as three drawn stars rather than a number. A completed level shows its best
  score. Locked levels show a lock; a level unlocks when the previous one is completed.

Draw the stars and the locks with the renderer's ink-and-parchment vocabulary, not with
text or emoji. Keep it navigable by keyboard, with real `<button>` elements and visible
focus.

## Stars in play

After a round, the result panel already reports the score. Add the stars: show which
threshold was reached, with the next threshold and how far away it is — "18,400 · two
stars · 3,000 more for three" tells a player what to do next, where three drawn stars do
not.

Level thresholds are in `levels.js` as `stars: [one, two, three]`, derived from bot play in
P5.8.

## Progress saving

- **Local first.** `localStorage`, keyed per level: best score, stars, completed. It must
  work with no account and no network, because that is how most people will play it.
- **Then the guard.** `POST /_guard/profile` with the same blob, and read it back on load,
  merging by best score per level so playing on a phone and a laptop cannot lose progress.
  The endpoint is documented in `docs/DEPLOY.md`; it is per-account and per-game and it
  returns nothing useful when there is no session, which must be handled silently rather
  than as an error.
- Never block the game on the network. If the guard is unreachable the game plays and
  syncs later.
