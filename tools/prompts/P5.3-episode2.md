## What this episode adds

Per `EPISODES` in `data.js`: `lob` and `spike` join the bag; Hogg and Helmet Hog join the
pigs; **stone** and **TNT crates** join the materials. Wood and glass carry over. Nothing
from Episode 3 or 4.

Teaching order across the 13:

| levels | job |
| --- | --- |
| 1–3 | stone. It is heavy and tough — the lesson is that dropping it is itself a weapon, and that hitting it directly is a waste |
| 4–6 | `spike`. Stone cores that only a hardened Spike gets through, and glass it passes straight through |
| 7–9 | TNT. Crates placed so a chain does the work, and at least one where detonating early wastes it |
| 10–12 | Hogg and Helmet Hog. The helmet ignores 60% from above, so a dropped roof is the wrong answer and a sideways collapse is the right one |
| 13 | the episode's biggest structure. Stone core, TNT, both new pigs, every critter needed |

Two things to get right because they are specific to this episode:

- **Stone changes the physics of a level, not just its hit points.** A stone slab on glass
  posts is a trap for the fortress, not a defence. Use that.
- **Helmet Hog's armour is directional.** A level whose only solution is dropping something
  on a Helmet Hog is a level that does not work. Make the sideways answer available and
  the vertical one insufficient.
